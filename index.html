nombook

your own little cookbook

Get it on the App Store

Nombook is a recipe diary. Write down the dishes you actually cook — a photo or one of the hand-drawn covers, the ingredients, the steps — then log each time you make it and how it turned out.

It is not a discovery app and it is not a social network. It is your cookbook, kept on your phone.

What it does

  • Write a recipe by hand, from a photo of a cookbook page, from a pasted block of text, or from a link.
  • Cook from it in a full-screen mode that keeps the screen awake, with a timer on any step that needs one.
  • Keep a diary — every cook, with a star rating, a note and photos of how it came out.
  • Plan the week and build a shopping list from the ingredients.
  • Find something to cook from what is actually in the fridge.
  • Send a recipe to a friend as a link. They keep their own copy.

Private by default

No accounts, no sign-up, no password. No ads, no subscriptions, no tracking. There is no Nombook server: your cookbook lives on your iPhone, and — if you leave iCloud Sync on — in your private iCloud, which we cannot read.

Read the privacy policy.
